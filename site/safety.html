Public-site disclosures
What is published

Only the static preview, sample data, design concepts and public safety statements are published.

What is not published

No JARVIS application code, database, logs, credentials, account identifiers or real application data are included.

How local controls differ

Actions shown as disabled in this static site are available only in the separate local JARVIS application, when applicable.

Emergency control concept: STOP ALL is intended to stop paper bots and disarm demo auto-trading. This website does not send that command.
Preview of the JARVIS project: sample data, demo accounts and paper trading only. Not financial advice. The indices JARVIS studies behave like random walks, so paper results do not show a trading edge.
