Research and paper trading for Deriv

A workshop for studying Deriv markets with a team of bots

JARVIS watches R_50, R_75 and R_100, lets research bots vote, and tests every strategy on a paper account. Real trading is disabled.

Open dashboardImport a bot
What it does

Research and analysis

EMA, RSI, momentum and volatility from live ticks, kept in a local SQLite memory.

Bot team and consensus

Bots vote and a risk gate blocks unsafe moments such as a stale feed. No single bot is in control.

Paper and demo trading

A virtual account tracks balance, drawdown and win rate. An opt-in demo trader is limited to R_50 and a small stake.

Strategy Lab

28 strategies catalogued and 10 running as paper bots, with backtests and walk-forward tests.
How it works

1. Collect

Live ticks arrive from Deriv's public feed.

2. Analyse

Indicators and strategies read the history.

3. Vote and check

Bots vote; the risk gate can say no.

4. Paper trade

Results are recorded and reviewed, never real money.
Built-in safety

Real trading is disabled

No real-money order exists in the code. Only demo accounts are used.

Imported bots are boxed in

They run in a container with no network and can only return rise, fall or hold.

STOP ALL always works

One button stops paper bots and disarms demo auto-trading.
